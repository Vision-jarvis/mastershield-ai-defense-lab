Explainability

Methodology

We red-teamed our own benchmark.

An early build of this system reported 100% recall and 1.0000 ROC-AUC. A leakage audit proved the result was the simulator labelling its own attacks: a depth-one decision stump on the character length of the account name reproduced the entire score. We found nine defects, fixed each in code, and re-measured. The figures on this page are what survived that.

    Known limitations

    P99 latency does not yet meet the 9.8 ms target. ADV_30 feedback poisoning is undetected at 0% recall and additionally passes our sanitisation filter, which is a complete end-to-end loop vulnerability. ADV_11 sub-perceptual biometric evasion sits at 11.1%. Serverless hosting cannot hold WebSockets, so this deployment polls /api/live_batch; local runs stream over a socket.

    Provenance

    Every figure on this page is read live from /api/benchmark and the taxonomy from the shipped configuration, so the console cannot drift from the measured artifact. All payment data is synthetic. No real cardholder, transaction or biometric data was used.
