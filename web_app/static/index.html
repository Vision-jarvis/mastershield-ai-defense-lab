Methodology

An early build of this system reported 100% recall and 1.0000 ROC-AUC. A leakage audit proved those numbers were the simulator labelling its own attacks: a depth-1 decision stump on account-name string length reproduced the entire result. We found nine defects, fixed each in code, and re-benchmarked honestly. The numbers above are what survived.

Known limitations, stated plainly. P99 latency does not yet meet the 9.8 ms target. ADV_30 feedback poisoning is undetected at 0% recall. ADV_11 sub-perceptual biometric evasion sits at 11.1%. Serverless hosting cannot hold WebSockets, so this deployment polls /api/live_batch; local runs stream over a socket.
All payment data is synthetic. No real cardholder, transaction, or biometric data was used.
